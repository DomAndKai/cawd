Roomie Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

Roomie helps roommates manage rent, utilities, chores, and general house coordination. This policy explains what information Roomie collects, how it is used, and how you can request deletion of your account.

Information We Collect

Roomie collects the information needed to create and operate your account, including your username, email address, Firebase authentication identifiers, and house membership. If you create a house, Roomie stores the house name and house code. Roomie does not ask for phone numbers during account signup.

Roomie also stores the content and activity you add inside a house, including chat messages, chores, chore frequency, chore assignments, rent tracker settings, rent splits, due dates, utility bills, paid or unpaid status, anonymous poke activity, in-app notifications, and general house coordination details.

Firebase and Authentication

Roomie uses Firebase as its backend. Roomie uses Firebase Authentication to create accounts and sign users in with email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. Firebase handles authentication credentials, including passwords for email/password accounts. Roomie does not store your plain-text password.

Roomie uses Cloud Firestore to store and sync app data, including account information, house data, chat messages, chores, bills, payment status, and notifications. Firebase may process technical information such as IP address, user agent, device information, and authentication activity to provide security, prevent abuse, and operate these services.

House-Shared Data

Information added to a shared house may be visible to other members of that house. This includes house membership, usernames, chat messages, chores, bills, rent settings, splits, due dates, payment status, and coordination details. Chat messages are not currently designed to be deleted by users.

Users join a house by entering a house code. The member who creates a house may be labeled as the house owner, but all house members can send chats, post chores, edit or delete chores, and post bills. Bill editing or deletion may be limited to the member who posted the bill.

Anonymous Pokes and Notifications

House members can send anonymous pokes to remind another member about chores, bills, rent, or other house responsibilities. Anonymous pokes are designed to be anonymous to other house members, but Roomie may retain technical records needed to deliver the notification, show it in the in-app notifications tab, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

Roomie provides an in-app notifications tab on iOS and web. If you enable iOS notifications, Roomie may send push notifications through Apple Push Notification service for anonymous pokes, chores, bills, rent due dates, and other house activity. Roomie does not currently plan to send browser push notifications for the web app.

Payment Handles and Payments

Roomie may let you optionally add Venmo or Zelle payment details so house members can pay the right person more easily. A Zelle identifier may be a phone number or email address if you choose to enter one for that purpose. These payment details may be visible or shared with house members as needed to support payment buttons and autofilled payment links.

Roomie does not process payments directly, store bank account information, or ask for Venmo, Zelle, or other payment-service passwords. Payment buttons may open Venmo or Zelle with the recipient and amount filled in, but the payment is completed through that third-party payment service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide Roomie features, create and manage accounts, authenticate users, sync house data, show house activity, track chores and bills, support payment links, send relevant notifications, troubleshoot issues, prevent misuse, improve the product, and respond to support requests.

No Sale of Personal Data

Roomie does not sell your personal data. Roomie does not currently use PostHog, Firebase Crashlytics, or similar analytics or crash reporting tools.

Account Deletion

You can initiate account deletion from the account menu in Roomie. You can also contact kaibottombot@gmail.com for help with deletion. Deletion requests may remove or de-identify your account information, subject to information Roomie needs to retain for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational reasons.

Because Roomie is built around shared house records, some content you added to a house may remain visible to other house members if it is needed to preserve the household record, such as existing chat messages, chores, bills, payment status, or rent history.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or account deletion, email kaibottombot@gmail.com.
