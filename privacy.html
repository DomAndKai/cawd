Cawd Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

Cawd helps roommates manage chores, rent, utilities, caws, and general house coordination. This policy explains what information Cawd collects, how it is used, and how you can request deletion of your account.

Information We Collect

Cawd collects the information needed to create and operate your account, including your username, email address, Firebase authentication identifiers, optional profile photo if you add one, and roost membership. If you create a roost, Cawd stores the roost name and roost code. Cawd does not ask for phone numbers during account signup.

Cawd also stores the content and activity you add inside a roost, including chat messages, chores, chore frequency, chore assignments, cover-and-complete activity, rent tracker settings, rent splits, due dates, utility bills, paid or unpaid status, anonymous caw activity, in-app notifications, and general house coordination details.

If you submit feedback in the app, Cawd may collect your feedback message, reply email, Firebase user ID, username, account email, roost ID, roost name, app version, platform, feedback status, and submission time.

Firebase and Authentication

Cawd uses Firebase as its backend. Cawd uses Firebase Authentication to create accounts and sign users in with email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. Firebase handles authentication credentials, including passwords for email/password accounts. Cawd does not store your plain-text password.

Cawd uses Cloud Firestore to store and sync app data, including account information, roost data, chat messages, chores, bills, payment status, caws, feedback, and notifications. Cawd uses Firebase Storage for profile photos, Firebase Messaging for push notifications, and Firebase Cloud Functions for server-side tasks such as feedback submission and account deletion. Firebase may process technical information such as IP address, user agent, device information, and authentication activity to provide security, prevent abuse, and operate these services.

Roost-Shared Data

Information added to a shared roost may be visible to other members of that roost. This includes roost membership, usernames, optional profile photos, chat messages, chores, bills, rent settings, splits, due dates, payment status, and coordination details. Chat messages are not currently designed to be deleted by users.

Users join a roost by entering a roost code. The member who creates a roost may be labeled as the owner, but all roost members can send chats, post chores, edit or delete chores, and post bills. Bill editing or deletion may be limited to the member who posted the bill.

Caws and Notifications

Roost members can caw at another member to remind them about a chore or other house responsibility. Caws are designed to be anonymous to other roost members, but Cawd may retain technical records needed to deliver the notification, show it in the in-app notifications tab, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

Cawd provides an in-app notifications tab on iOS, and plans to provide one on web when the web app is available. If you enable iOS notifications, Cawd may send push notifications through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Messaging for caws, chores, bills, rent due dates, and other house activity. Cawd stores notification tokens connected to your user account so notifications can be delivered and managed. Cawd does not currently plan to send browser push notifications for the web app.

Profile Photos and Permissions

If you choose to add a profile photo, Cawd requests access to your photo library so you can select an image. Profile photos are uploaded to Firebase Storage and the photo URL is saved in your user profile and roost member record so other roost members can see it.

Cawd does not access contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, health data, advertising identifiers, or IDFA tracking data based on the current app design.

Payment Handles and Smart Pay

Cawd may let you optionally add Venmo or Zelle payment details so roost members can pay the right person more easily. A Zelle identifier may be a phone number or email address if you choose to enter one for that purpose. These payment details may be visible or shared with roost members as needed to support payment buttons and autofilled payment links.

Cawd does not process payments directly, store bank account information, or ask for Venmo, Zelle, or other payment-service passwords. Smart pay buttons may open Venmo with the recipient, note, and amount filled in, or help copy Zelle details, but the payment is completed through that third-party payment service.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide Cawd features, create and manage accounts, authenticate users, sync roost data, show house activity, track chores and bills, support smart pay links, send relevant notifications, troubleshoot issues, prevent misuse, improve the product, review feedback, and respond to support requests.

Analytics

Cawd uses PostHog to understand app usage and improve the product. PostHog may collect usage events, device or app information, platform information, timestamps, and identifiers that may be linked to your account depending on how the app is configured. Cawd does not use PostHog for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

No Sale of Personal Data

Cawd does not sell your personal data. Cawd does not currently use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics, IDFA, or advertising-data collection based on the current app design.

Account Deletion

You can initiate account deletion from the account menu in Cawd. You can also contact cawdsupport@gmail.com for help with deletion. Account deletion runs through a Firebase Cloud Function and removes your Firebase Authentication account, Firestore user profile, username reservation, profile photo file, push notification token records, active roost member entries, and your user ID from roost member lists. Payment handles stored in active roost membership records are removed when those membership records are removed.

Shared roost data is not deleted during account deletion. Chores, bills, rent records, chat messages, feedback submissions, and other shared house history may remain so other roost members do not lose shared records. Some remaining shared records may still contain your previous display name, user ID, or historical action data.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or account deletion, email cawdsupport@gmail.com.
