Cawd Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Cawd helps roommates manage chores, rent, utilities, caws, and general house coordination. This policy explains what information Cawd collects, how it is used, and how you can request deletion of your account.

Information We Collect

Cawd collects the information needed to create and operate your account, including your username, email address, Firebase authentication identifiers, optional profile photo if you add one, and roost membership. If you create a roost, Cawd stores the roost name and roost code. Cawd does not ask for phone numbers during account signup.

Cawd also stores the content and activity you add inside a roost, including chat messages, chores, chore frequency, chore assignments, cover-and-complete activity, rent tracker settings, rent splits, due dates, utility bills, paid or unpaid status, anonymous caw activity, in-app notifications, and general house coordination details.

Firebase and Authentication

Cawd uses Firebase as its backend. Cawd uses Firebase Authentication to create accounts and sign users in with email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. Firebase handles authentication credentials, including passwords for email/password accounts. Cawd does not store your plain-text password.

Cawd uses Cloud Firestore to store and sync app data, including account information, roost data, chat messages, chores, bills, payment status, caws, and notifications. Firebase may process technical information such as IP address, user agent, device information, and authentication activity to provide security, prevent abuse, and operate these services.

Roost-Shared Data

Information added to a shared roost may be visible to other members of that roost. This includes roost membership, usernames, optional profile photos, chat messages, chores, bills, rent settings, splits, due dates, payment status, and coordination details. Chat messages are not currently designed to be deleted by users.

Users join a roost by entering a roost code. The member who creates a roost may be labeled as the owner, but all roost members can send chats, post chores, edit or delete chores, and post bills. Bill editing or deletion may be limited to the member who posted the bill.

Caws and Notifications

Roost members can caw at another member to remind them about a chore or other house responsibility. Caws are designed to be anonymous to other roost members, but Cawd may retain technical records needed to deliver the notification, show it in the in-app notifications tab, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

Cawd provides an in-app notifications tab on iOS, and plans to provide one on web when the web app is available. If you enable iOS notifications, Cawd may send push notifications through Apple Push Notification service for caws, chores, bills, rent due dates, and other house activity. Cawd does not currently plan to send browser push notifications for the web app.

Payment Handles and Smart Pay

Cawd may let you optionally add Venmo or Zelle payment details so roost members can pay the right person more easily. A Zelle identifier may be a phone number or email address if you choose to enter one for that purpose. These payment details may be visible or shared with roost members as needed to support payment buttons and autofilled payment links.

Cawd does not process payments directly, store bank account information, or ask for Venmo, Zelle, or other payment-service passwords. Smart pay buttons may open Venmo with the recipient, note, and amount filled in, or help copy Zelle details, but the payment is completed through that third-party payment service.

Feedback Board

Cawd links to a feedback board hosted by UserJot. If you submit feedback, vote, or interact with that board, the information you provide may be handled by UserJot according to its own terms and privacy practices. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the feedback board.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide Cawd features, create and manage accounts, authenticate users, sync roost data, show house activity, track chores and bills, support smart pay links, send relevant notifications, troubleshoot issues, prevent misuse, improve the product, and respond to support requests.

No Sale of Personal Data

Cawd does not sell your personal data. Cawd does not currently use PostHog, Firebase Crashlytics, or similar analytics or crash reporting tools.

Account Deletion

You can initiate account deletion from the account menu in Cawd. You can also contact kaibottombot@gmail.com for help with deletion. Deletion requests may remove or de-identify your account information, subject to information Cawd needs to retain for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational reasons.

Because Cawd is built around shared roost records, some content you added to a roost may remain visible to other roost members if it is needed to preserve the household record, such as existing chat messages, chores, bills, payment status, or rent history.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or account deletion, email kaibottombot@gmail.com.
